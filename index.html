Send to Garmin watch

Download the route as a Garmin .fit course file, then transfer it to your watch:

  1. Tap Download .fit below.
  2. Open Garmin Connect (mobile or web).
  3. Go to Training → Courses → Import and choose the file.
  4. Save the course, then tap Send to Device.
  5. Wait ~30 s for the watch to sync over Bluetooth.
  6. On the watch: Navigate → Courses, pick the route, start.
Technical notes
  • Sport is set to walking — appears under that filter on the watch.
  • Turn cues are embedded as course points. Map-capable watches (Fenix, Epix, Forerunner 255+) will show turn-by-turn prompts; older watches show a breadcrumb with waypoint chimes.
  • Round-trip routes loop back to the start; the watch shows the full loop.
  • USB shortcut: with the watch plugged in, drop the .fit straight into GARMIN/NewFiles/ — skips Garmin Connect entirely.
  • Heads-up: if the route shows dead-end spurs, the watch may give "off course" alerts when you skip them. These are guidance only, not blocking — you can mute or ignore them.
